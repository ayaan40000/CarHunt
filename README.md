# CarHunt — Reliable Car Under $15K Dashboard

Ayaan Pathan's used-car search dashboard: a filterable, single-file board of Houston-area listings, with an expert pursue/avoid workup, an Android Auto by year/trim matrix, and a VIN / inspection / scam guide. Every deal is graded A+ to F against a market value estimate. Updated every Thursday, with daily incremental sweeps. Reworked Nov 4, 2026: compact cards with collapsible details, a body-type filter, a slim control deck, a Texas paperwork tab, and the budget raised to $15,000 (see the headline below).

**Live:** https://ayaan40000.github.io/CarHunt/

**Data stamp:** Listings from Facebook Marketplace sweeps, Nov 19, 2026 — every board and staged record re-verified live, fresh same-day estimates run for the 10 newly admitted cars (the other 47 estimates are carried from Nov 18, marked on their cards), Phase 3 gates applied. 2 checked listings were dead and dropped; 24 checked listings were excluded on live evidence (branded titles, excluded engines, compact hatchbacks). Three full VINs found in the published data were redacted. Estimates are medians of the providers named on each card — four of the new estimates include a CarMax comparable asking price, named on their cards; one carried estimate (the 2019 Fusion SEL) includes a CarMax recent offer, named as an offer on its card; no other estimate includes a CarMax figure. Listings sell fast; a car shown here may already be gone.

## Owner's criteria

- Under $15,000 cash, no financing (private / non-dealer sellers preferred) — budget raised from $12,000 on 2026-11-04
- Any car type — no trucks, no compact hatchbacks
- At most 100,000 miles
- Must have: factory Android Auto, reverse camera
- Preferred: gas, hybrid — Not preferred: diesel, electric, manual transmission
- Reliability first; every model reliability cross-checked; models with reliability concerns or major recalls are excluded
- Every deal graded A+ to F (A+ = great deal at a great price) against a CarMax / leading-provider value estimate — target is below estimated value

Honest headline (Nov 19, 2026): **47 of 57 listings meet all must-haves** — led by a 2018 Chevrolet Malibu 1LT in Houston ($5,000, 85,000 mi, grade A, 51.5% below estimate) and the 2024 Chevrolet Malibu LT in Katy ($9,799, 52,000 mi, grade A, 46.4% below estimate). Two 2020 Malibus move from capped to meets after the federal backup-camera rule is applied board-wide. Fresh finds: two 2023 Fortes cut $2,100 and $1,900 the same day they were found. One car is priced above its estimate (a 2019 Elantra Limited, grade D-). 2024–25 Elantras are held back under the Phase 3 rule — they are not scored yet. Four of the new estimates include a CarMax comparable asking price, named on their cards; one carried estimate (the 2019 Fusion SEL) includes a CarMax recent offer, named as an offer; no other estimate includes a CarMax figure.

Key dashboard rules: Android Auto on each card is the expert matrix verdict for that year/trim (not the seller's claim — mismatches are flagged), the underpriced flag is measured against the market estimate (median of KBB, CarGurus, Edmunds and dealer comparable figures, named per record), and every card shows its A+ to F grade from the rubric in `docs/METHOD.md`.

## How it updates

A refresh re-runs the Facebook Marketplace sweep via the Pi browser only (see `docs/METHOD.md`), replaces `data/listings.json` with the new sweep only, regenerates the dashboard, updates the data stamp date, and republishes the files to this repo. Every publish passes the Phase 0 publish gate in `docs/METHOD.md` first: duplicates are reconciled by Marketplace item ID, conflicts are adjudicated against the expert guide, and every card shows its open blockers. Since Phase 3 (2026-11-17), Carvana search results are also screened as a supplementary source; Carvana candidates are staged as sourced-unverified and are re-verified in a live browser on Thursdays before they can ship. Since 2026-11-18 the data flows through the SQLite store in `data/carhunt.db`: staging is imported into the DB, the board is edited there, `data/listings.json` is exported from it, and the export must pass the round-trip verify before the dashboard is rebuilt. Research only — no sellers are contacted and nothing is purchased.
